# Code of conduct

agenttail follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Don't post other people's private information, and don't paste session logs, tokens or paths that identify someone.
- Assume good faith and help newcomers.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer, [@ionut27001](https://github.com/ionut27001), on GitHub. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who keep breaking the rules.
